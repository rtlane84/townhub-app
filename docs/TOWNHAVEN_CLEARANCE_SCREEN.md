# TownHaven preliminary name screen

**Screen date:** August 9, 2026  
**Product:** U.S.-focused local-business discovery, marketplace, ordering, appointment-request, and business-operations software  
**Decision owner:** LaneTech LLC  
**Decision:** Proceed with the TownHaven rebrand while accepting the residual risk described below.

## Important limitation

This is a no-cost preliminary screen, not legal advice, a legal opinion, or a guarantee that TownHaven is available to use or register. It does not replace a comprehensive search by a U.S.-licensed trademark attorney. LaneTech LLC should revisit professional review and possible federal registration when finances permit.

The USPTO explains that a search must consider marks that look alike, sound alike, have similar meanings or commercial impressions, and cover related goods or services. An exact-name search alone is not sufficient. See [USPTO federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) and [comprehensive clearance searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks).

## Searches performed

- Official USPTO Trademark Search, exact combined word `TOWNHAVEN`: zero live or dead records observed.
- USPTO and ordinary-web searches for `TownHaven`, `Town Haven`, spelling variants, software, mobile apps, marketplaces, local commerce, and community platforms.
- Indexed Apple App Store and Google Play results for TownHaven.
- West Virginia business and ordinary company-use searches for TownHaven and Town Haven.
- Domain and general web searches for `townhaven.io`, `TownHaven.com`, and exact-name business use.

## Findings

- No directly competing local-business marketplace or software service using the exact TownHaven name was found in the searches above.
- No relevant indexed Apple App Store or Google Play listing using the exact name was found.
- No matching West Virginia business result was found.
- Unrelated uses include real-estate/place names, a small Roblox role-playing game, and fictional/location references.
- `TownHaven.com` was listed for resale and was not observed operating a competing service.
- `HAVEN` is a crowded term in federal records, including some software-related marks. None reviewed appeared to identify a local-business marketplace under the combined TownHaven name, but the crowded field creates residual uncertainty.
- `townhaven.io` returned no DNS records during the screen. This is not proof of availability; availability must be confirmed and purchased through a registrar before launch work is treated as release-ready.

## Release gates and repeat search

Before public release:

1. Confirm and purchase `townhaven.io` through the selected registrar.
2. Confirm that App Store Connect permits `TownHaven` as the next version's app name.
3. Repeat the exact USPTO, app-store, West Virginia business, and ordinary-web searches.
4. Record the repeat-search date and any new potentially related result below.
5. Stop the release and reassess if a new live or actively used confusingly similar mark is found for marketplace, advertising, ordering, directory, community-platform, or SaaS services.

## Logo provenance

Before release, LaneTech LLC must confirm and record who created the artwork, the source files, and the applicable assignment or license. Do not assume the wordmark search clears the logo design.
